Wow. Just got my Pebble Index 01. I’ve been excited about this device since it was announced, and a bit concerned as well as to whether it’d work.

Well, I love it.

I even bought the 3d printed sizing kit to make sure it’d work—I opted for the size that fits on my ring finger, despite them suggesting that I go with the index.

After a quick setup (and a slightly buggy vibe coded iOS app—Pebble if you’re interested I’m happy to help improve the UI of the app), my first two tests performed perfectly.

And here’s the thing—the Pebble, in one instant, with one try, did something that Apple Watch and iPhone and Homepod have all failed utterly at over the years. I added a reminder, and had an instant, 100% hit rate of success.

That’s a great product.

I asked it to remind me to pickup milk tonight, and a second later I checked and saw it in my Apple Reminders app.

I can’t tell you how many times I’ve tried to get my Apple Watch to performance this dance. It often has a 50% success rate, and that’s infuriating. Sometimes Siri takes a nap (and yes, I have the new Siri on iOS 27, if anything it’s worse than old siri for simple tasks like this), sometimes it misreads me and plays a song, and sometimes it just errors out.

The Pebble is so simple, and perfect.

I’m excited to see if it becomes my replacement for—well many things. So much of the reason I hold onto an Apple Watch (apart from seeing calendar events) is to be able to quickly capture 5 second ideas for later. With the Index 01 I can press a button, speak into it, and then have my phone process that once I get back to it.

Now, this glowing report is obviously a bit new, since I just tried it. But if it works as well as I’m hoping, I’ll be an absolute believer.

What if I don’t have 750 words in me? Years ago I gave myself a challenge, to write 1,000 words a day, six days a week. I did that, more or less, for a few years. It was a wonderful experience, and gave me the necessary muscles to be able to put out words and challenge myself.

But some days I didn’t have it in me, so I would just journal; trashing words together like an ape on a keyboard.

Something I’ve often wondered, in the time since, is what I should do if I don’t have the minimum amount of words in me on a given day. Some days I combat that by getting it out. But lately, many days that’s meant not writing at all. I don’t love that. But I also don’t want to put out rubbish to people who pay attention with their time.

Enter this blog. I have lots of shorter thoughts I want to share on a daily basis; but many aren't processed enough to fit into a blog post, or worthy enough of an email blast. That's what this is for. It's my own replacement to Twitter of old. It will be an endless stream of consciousness, hopefully just a little better quality than my journal entries of old.
